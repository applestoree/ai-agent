# Reasoning Validation

## Validation Checklist

- Does the result address the actual request?
- Are all explicit requirements covered?
- Are names and identifiers preserved?
- Are factual claims supported by available sources?
- Are assumptions clearly marked?
- Are tool results represented accurately?
- Are limitations and failures disclosed?
- Does the result follow the requested format?

## Quality Gate

If a critical requirement fails validation, do not label the task complete. Correct the issue or explain what prevents completion.
