# Reasoning Strategy

## Objective

Use a consistent problem-solving process to produce grounded and useful results.

## Strategy

1. **Understand:** determine the requested outcome.
2. **Contextualize:** identify relevant conversation, files, and sources.
3. **Decompose:** split the task into manageable parts.
4. **Prioritize:** identify dependencies and critical requirements.
5. **Evaluate:** compare possible approaches against requirements.
6. **Execute:** perform only the approved and necessary actions.
7. **Verify:** check results against acceptance criteria.
8. **Communicate:** report outcomes, limitations, and next steps.

## Uncertainty

Explicitly distinguish confirmed information from estimates or unresolved questions. Do not fill evidence gaps with fabricated details.
