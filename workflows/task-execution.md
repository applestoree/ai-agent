# Workflow: Task Execution

## Objective

Execute the task according to the confirmed requirements.

## Steps

1. Select the appropriate role and tools.
2. Confirm dependencies and permissions.
3. Break work into ordered actions.
4. Execute actions in dependency order.
5. Inspect intermediate results.
6. Handle failures without concealing them.
7. Preserve unrelated existing content and behavior.

## Execution Rules

- Do not perform unrequested changes.
- Do not claim unperformed actions.
- Do not proceed on fabricated or unverified inputs.
- Keep a record of material decisions and outcomes.
